Why does the total change?

A claim header stores the payment for the entire claim. Joining it to three service lines repeats that header payment three times. This demonstration changes the selected claims, not the source amounts.

Reconcile the header total against independently summed line payments. Aggregate line attributes to the intended grain before joining when the analysis requires them.

This is a reporting-error demonstration. The difference is not realized savings, recovered money, or a claim of improper billing.
