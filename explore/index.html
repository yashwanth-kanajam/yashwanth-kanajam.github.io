Header-to-line reconciliation

Select claim groups from the synthetic dataset to see how repeating header payments changes the reported total.

Why the total changes

Each claim header stores the payment for the whole claim. Joining that header to three service lines repeats its payment three times. Selecting claim groups changes which claims are counted. The source amounts stay the same.

Check the header total against the line payments summed separately. For a claim-level result, aggregate line payments to one row per claim before joining. When keeping line-level detail, do not sum the repeated header payment.

The difference is a reporting error in synthetic data and does not represent money saved.

Source: synthetic claims dataset, generated with seed 17 for reproducibility. Payment amounts are stored as whole cents.
