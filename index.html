WAPE (weighted absolute percentage error) is total absolute error divided by total demand. Change is the relative change in error, so a negative value means lower error. Qtrs is the number of quarters, out of 12, in which the corrected forecast had lower error. PNM and PSCO are left out because their forecast and demand definitions could not be confirmed.

Grid operator names

SOCO: Southern Company Services · PJM: PJM Interconnection · ISNE: ISO New England · TVA: Tennessee Valley Authority · BPAT: Bonneville Power Administration · AECI: Associated Electric Cooperative. EIA calls these balancing authorities.
